# Zhang et al. 2026, ScaleEarth (GSD-gated LoRA with an uncertainty-driven fallback)

## Problem
RS images range from centimetres to tens of metres per pixel, but RS VLMs either ignore ground sampling distance (GSD) or pass it as a text token, using the same weights at every scale.

## Approach
- Backbone Qwen3-VL-8B. Stage 1 fully fine-tunes it on RS-GPT4V; Stage 2 freezes it (bf16, or 4-bit NF4 for the efficiency variant) and trains only adapters and a scale head (Sec 3.1, 3.2).
- CS-HLoRA: a single rank-64 LoRA in all LLM attention and FFN projections whose rank dimensions are switched by sigmoid gates of s = log10(GSD); thresholds start at object (ranks 0 to 10, active at 1 m or finer), structure (11 to 31, active at 10 m or finer) and semantic (32 to 63, always on) scales and remain learnable.
- SSE-U: a heteroscedastic head on mean-pooled ViT features predicting a Gaussian over log-GSD, trained with negative log-likelihood on samples with exact GSD.
- Inference rule (Sec 3.3, eq 5): use caller metadata if present; else use the SSE-U mean if its standard deviation is below 0.3 in log10 units (about a factor of 2); else fall back to 1.0 m, chosen so a wrong fallback keeps the structure and semantic ranks active. The authors describe the use-or-abstain decision as calibrated by the model itself. Overhead under 2% over base inference.
- GeoScale-VQA: 1.5M QA pairs over 0.06 to 10 m GSD (772K high tier, 102K mid, 201K low-A, 411K low-B), combining GSD-recovered RSVQA, RSVQAxBEN and GeoLLaVA-8K data with new questions generated by Qwen3-VL-32B on PatternNet and MtSCCD, filtered by Claude Opus 4.6 as a judge (Sec 2).

## Data and benchmarks
XLRS-Bench (13 VQA sub-tasks, ultra-high resolution) and OmniEarth-Bench (7 Earth-science spheres, multiple choice). Neither provides GSD, so every ScaleEarth score uses the SSE-U estimate or the fallback (Sec 4.1).

## Key results
- XLRS-Bench average 59.4, 5.2 points above the strongest RS specialist (GeoEyes); the 4-bit variant scores 57.4 (Table 1, Appendix A.2).
- OmniEarth-Bench average 40.71, 7.45 points above InternVL3-72B and 11.64 above Claude-3.7-Sonnet (Table 2, Appendix A.2).
- SSE-U: log10 MAE 0.12 on held-out exact-GSD samples; its 68% and 95% intervals cover 74% and 96% of targets; the fallback triggers on 8.4% of benchmark samples and disabling it costs 0.7 points (Sec 4.1).
- Leave-one-out on XLRS-Bench-lite (Fig 6): vanilla LoRA instead of CS-HLoRA minus 4.2; masking GSD fields in the data minus 3.8; random tier initialisation minus 3.5; removing SSE-U minus 1.4.
- Cost (Appendix A.2): 4-bit model runs the benchmark on a single A100 in 3 hours; served as W4A16 in vLLM it has about 2.5x lower peak memory than bf16. Training ran on 16 A100s.

## Limitations
- Uncertainty covers only the resolution estimate; answers carry no confidence, and there is no answer-level abstention. The conclusion names extending it to answer uncertainty as future work.
- Training data is LLM generated and LLM filtered, so quality is bounded by the generator.
- Tested only on Qwen3-VL-8B and on GSDs within 0.062 to 10 m; 30 m and coarser products were not evaluated (Appendix F).
- Code and weights not yet released.

## What it means for EvidEO
- Adopt: the three-branch rule (trusted metadata first, then a model estimate only if its predicted interval is tight, else a safe default) is a clean template for EvidEO's abstention logic, applied to the instrument's output rather than to metadata. Report interval coverage the way they do (nominal vs empirical).
- Adopt: EvidEO should always pass true scene metadata (pixel spacing, sensor) instead of letting any model guess it, which this paper shows matters.
- Beat: ScaleEarth confirms the gap: even the most uncertainty-aware RS VLM of 2026 gives no calibrated confidence on the answer and no provenance; EvidEO's per-answer calibrated confidence plus abstain remains novel.
- Note for CPU: even its 4-bit version is served on A100 GPUs, so EvidEO's small LoRA parser must stay far smaller.

## Full-text check (2026-10-14)
Ingredients (paper's own system as built and evaluated):
- C1 Calibrated per-answer confidence: No. Calibration (interval coverage 74% and 96%, Sec 4.1; calibration ratio, Appendix A Fig 8) is reported for the GSD estimate only; answers carry no probability.
- C2 Threshold abstention with a next step: No for answers. There is a stated threshold (sigma below 0.3) that declines the GSD estimate and substitutes a default (Sec 3.3), but the model still answers and the user is told nothing.
- C3 Scene receipts that reproduce: No. Diagnostic GSD spoofing sweeps exist (Sec 3.3), but no scene IDs or re-run checks.
- C4 SAR chosen automatically under cloud: No. RGB optical imagery only.
- C5 CPU or offline deployment: No. A100 GPUs for training and inference; the 4-bit variant still targets a single 40 GB GPU.
- C6 Plain-language questions for non-experts: part. Free-form VQA, but evaluated on expert benchmarks in multiple-choice format; no non-expert study.
